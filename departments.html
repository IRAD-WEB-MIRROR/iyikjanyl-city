Paying for Water and Rubbish

Pay at the City Hall cash desk (room 2, Mon-Fri 9:00-12:00), at the post office or at the Talasbank branch on Bazar Street. Talasbank card holders can pay in Talasbank Online.

Associations

Associations that want to work with the City Hall (volunteers for the Feast, help for families) can get advice on registration and grants from Köpürö, the resource centre for associations in Talas.
