Welcome to Iyık Janyl!


Cathedral Hill above the city. Photo: Irad Wiki Commons

Dear visitors! On behalf of the City Hall and the people of Iyık Janyl, the Holy City of Irad and the mother see of the Syro-Iradi Catholic Church, we welcome you to the official website of our city. On our website you can find information about our city, its history, the Feast of St. Janyl and the services of the City Hall.
