The Mayor

Bakhtiyar Nazarov has been Mayor of Iyık Janyl since 2021 (appointed by the Governor).

He was born in Iyık Janyl in 1970 and studied geography at Talas State University (1992). He was a geography teacher in Iyık Janyl from 1992 to 2003, worked in the city education office from 2003 to 2012, and was deputy mayor for social affairs from 2012 to 2021. He is married with four children.

Reception of citizens: every Wednesday, 10:00-12:00, City Hall, room 5. Please register with the secretary.
